Self-Titled

A darkwave debut balancing melancholy, politics, and romantic decay.

FFO: Belgrado, The Cure, Siouxsie and the Banshees, She Past Away, Joy Division, Vision Video, Xmal Deutschland

An unashamedly gothic collection of songs and poems.

These 10 songs and poems revel in strangely optimistic melancholy, politicised undercurrents, queer awakenings, and romantic memory.
