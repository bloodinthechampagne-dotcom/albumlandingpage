Self-Titled

“Stirring, tricksy album from Melbourne’s excellent Blood in the Champagne.” — Mick Mercer

FFO: Belgrado, The Cure, Siouxsie and the Banshees, She Past Away, Joy Division, Vision Video, Xmal Deutschland

An unashamedly gothic collection of songs and poems.

It revels in strangely optimistic melancholy, politicised undercurrents, queer awakenings, and romantic memory.
